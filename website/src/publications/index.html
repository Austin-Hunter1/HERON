Results: first flights, Boulder Reservoir, 16 Nov 2026

Three collects on the L5 band, with the nadir LHCP antenna: two hovers over open water (171756 and 172026) and one survey across the shoreline (172251). The direct signals are tracked closed-loop; the reflections are tracked open-loop from the geometry. These are preliminary results; processing continues.
