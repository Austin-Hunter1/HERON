HERON

Hydrological Estimation via Reflectometry from an Overflying Navigation-signal Receiver

We map surface water from a drone, using GPS, Galileo and BeiDou signals that bounce off the ground.

About the project Try the mission planner Learn GNSS
Where we fly

From Boulder Reservoir to the high lakes of the Front Range

How it works

Two antennas, one clock, a lot of radio samples

Fly

The drone flies a plan that puts the reflections of each satellite on the water.

Record

An up-looking and a down-looking antenna feed four radios that share one clock.

Compare

On the ground, the reflected signal is compared with the direct one, satellite by satellite.

Map

Strong, coherent reflections mark open water. Each one is placed at its point on the ground.
