Reflection windows on water

A window is a time span where the specular point is inside a mapped water body (same test as the flight planner) and terrain does not block the satellite, the reflected leg or the incoming leg. Incidence angle = 90° − elevation, from the ground normal. Water share = part of the first Fresnel zone over water (mean). Highlighted rows are active at the clock time. Click a row to jump to its start.
