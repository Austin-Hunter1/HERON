HERON · GNSS-RSignal processing course
HERON team session · about 90 minutes

From forest chimes to water reflections

We start with a person lost in a forest, listening to chimes. Then we build up everything a GNSS receiver must do to turn faint radio noise into a position. We finish with the geometry that lets HERON measure water from a drone. Every figure is live. Move the sliders while we talk. The tracking and acquisition results come from the HERON 20261013_Jiawei_Testing_x2 collect.

Built from docs/teaching/src/ with docs/teaching/build.py. Equations follow Morton et al., Position, Navigation, and Timing Technologies in the 21st Century, Ch. 15 (2021), and the public ICDs (IS-GPS-200, IS-GPS-705, IS-GPS-800, Galileo OS SIS ICD, BDS-SIS-ICD B1C/B2a). Real data: HERON tracking and acquisition outputs, collect 20261013_Jiawei_Testing_x2.
