| D-025 | 2026-09-24 | **Clock distribution board (revises the 10 MHz source of D-024):** a HERON board makes the common 10 MHz from a SiTime SiT5155 Super-TCXO (SiT5155AI-FK-33E0-10.000000, ±0.5 ppm, free-running) and sends it to all four SDRs through a 1:4 LMK1C1104 buffer. Each 10 MHz output has a DC block, a 5th-order 50 Ω low-pass filter (fc ≈ 13 MHz) and a 3 dB pad: about +7 dBm sine into 50 Ω. The same board takes one PPS (SMA) from a GNSS receiver and buffers it to four 3.3 V PPS outputs. Power: 5 V from the payload rail on a locking JST-GH connector. PCB: 4-layer, 1.6 mm, 84 × 56 mm, four M3 holes, edge-launch SMAs grouped by signal (10 MHz on one long edge, PPS on the other, PPS in and 5 V on a short edge). Assembly: bare boards from a fab, in-house pick-and-place and reflow. Design files: `Hardware/clock_board/`. | The low-pass filter keeps square-wave harmonics (1170/1180 MHz, 1570/1580 MHz) out of the recorded L5/L1 bands. The 10 MHz is not GPS-disciplined: the four SDRs stay coherent with each other, not with GPS time. The PPS source (GNSS receiver model) is still open. SiT5155: water-soluble flux only, no ultrasonic cleaning. Run ERC in KiCad before ordering (the generator used KiCad 7 CLI, which has no ERC). |
| D-026 | 2026-09-28 | **Clock board TCXO stays fixed-frequency (refines D-025):** the SiT5155 on the clock board is the TCXO version (SiT5155AI-FK-33E0-10.000000; the X suffix only sets a 250-piece reel). SiTime sets its frequency, supply voltage, output type and pin 1 function at the factory. It has no I2C interface, and nobody can program it after it leaves the factory. Pin 1 is output enable (10 kΩ pull-up, on by default). Pins 2, 3, 5, 7, 8 and 10 are NC and connect to GND. The board has no I2C connection. Layout change in rev A: C8 (100 nF) moves directly next to U3 pin 9 (VDD), and three more GND vias go at the U3 GND/NC pads (datasheet Layout Guidelines). | The I2C-tunable version (DCTCXO, same package) could let the NUC steer the 10 MHz to GPS. HERON does not use it. To change to it later, the board needs a new part number, SCL/SDA routed from pins 2 and 10 with pull-ups, and a strap on A0 (pin 5). |
| D-027 | 2026-10-05 | **Clock board rev B: SMP blind-mate to the SDRs (revises the rev B jumpers):** J3–J10 change from vertical SMA jacks to Amphenol RF SMP-MSSB-PCT10T (SMP male, smooth bore; through-hole legs, surface-mount signal tab; 2026-10-07, chosen over the SMP-MSSB-PCT for cost). J2 (PPS IN) stays an SMA (Amphenol 132134), because it takes a cable from the GNSS receiver. Each SDR rear 10 MHz and PPS SMA gets one adapter, Cinch/Johnson 134-1019-451 (SMA plug to SMP female). The adapter plugs directly onto the board jack when the SDR tray slides in. There is no bullet (2026-10-07: the user rejected a two-piece adapter-plus-bullet chain; the direct mate has less float, so the mate itself is the axial stop and the rear stop is a backup). There are no jumpers. The board does not float. The jacks sit in oversize plated holes (±0.20 mm float). They are soldered from the back with the four SDRs mated, so each jack aligns to its own SDR. The rack is made tighter, and each SDR tray gets its own axial preload. The schematic, netlist and pin-to-net map do not change; only the J3–J10 footprint and MPN change. KiCad 10 is now the tool for this board. Rack: the clock board sits on 20 mm standoffs, so the rear plate and the SSD bay stay back (`SSD_Y0` 128.4 mm) and the NUC cables keep a 20.8 mm crossing gap behind the NUC (2026-10-07; the SSD cage fills the full height, so cables cannot pass over or under it). The rear plate has access holes so a hex key reaches each rear stop screw from behind with everything installed. | Footprint dimensions come from the Amphenol SMP-MSSB-PCT10T outline drawing rev A. The jacks are hand-soldered: legs from the back with the SDRs mated, then the tab on top. Values to measure or confirm: Q-015. |
| D-028 | 2026-10-07 | **Clock board rev C: larger small parts for in-house assembly (refines D-027):** rev C is rev B with the smallest parts one size larger. All 49 0402 resistors and capacitors become 0603 (same values and makers). The nine TPD1E05U06 ESD diodes change from the X1SON package (DPYR, 0.6 x 1 mm) to the SOD-523 package (DYAR, 1.6 x 0.8 mm) of the same TI part, with the same pinout. The circuit, nets, board outline (118 x 44 mm), holes and jack positions do not change. Design files: `Hardware/clock_board_revC/` (a new folder; rev B stays as it is). | The user chose the scope (0402 and the ESD diodes; the 0603 parts stay 0603), a fixed outline and a new folder. The filter columns and the PPS output chains were placed again to fit the larger parts. DRC (0 errors) and ERC (0 errors) pass. Not decided: which revision the team orders. The rack model still reads rev B. The Digi-Key numbers and stock of the new MPNs are not checked yet. |
